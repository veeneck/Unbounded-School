Unbounded School

A flexible, mastery-based approach for one student at a time. Real progress, real projects, and room to breathe.

The Idea

Most school days are built around a fixed schedule and a fixed pace. We build around the student: what she actually needs to master, how her energy works that day, and what she is curious about right now.

Core academics happen first and finish earlier than a traditional day. The remaining time is protected for writing, projects, movement, and the kinds of work that do not fit neatly into a worksheet.

Progress is measured by completion and demonstrated skill—not by seat time. High test scores are a byproduct, not the point.

How a Day Works

Mornings focus on math, reading, and language with adaptive tools (Khan Academy, Lexia, Beast Academy). Afternoons open into science, projects, writing cycles, PE with neighbors, music, or deep work on something she chose.

Times flex with energy and season. Hard subjects are not stacked. Writing has a clear weekly process that ends with something finished and shared.

Quests & Writing

Quests are multi-step, real-world projects (baking, coding tools, creative challenges) that sit outside the core curriculum but still produce finished work and skills.

Writing is treated as a craft with a cycle: draft → feedback (often with Grok) → revise → share. Finished pieces live in the writing log.
