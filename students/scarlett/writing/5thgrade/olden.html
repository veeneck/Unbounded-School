Olden

A trip to Olden, Norway.

The Piece

When we went on our Norway cruise, one of our stops was Olden. I chose to write about this stop because of its beauty. It was the most beautiful place I have ever been. The fjords were stunning.

While we were there we went hiking in the mountains. We rode a gondola that took us super high before we started our hike. It was very tiring but worth it. The view was outstanding! We got to see the ocean, the cute little town, and smaller mountains all from above! The top seemed like it would never come. Our legs ached. My parents wanted to give up, but I kept them going. As we got closer to the top we saw snow and had a snowball fight, and our dad let us throw one at him. Then finally we made it to the top! We were in a cloud! The cloud felt like mist and looked like fog, it was so refreshing. The top was pretty quiet and peaceful, but quite chilly. I felt relieved and proud of myself.

Once we made it back down, we ate a popsicle and went to explore the town. There were so many old little houses and we got awesome photos. Fun fact: Olden has a legend about trolls, so there’s lot’s of troll statues. I would love to go back there some day.
