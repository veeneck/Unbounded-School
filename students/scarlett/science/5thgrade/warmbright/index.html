The Kit

A Science Unlocked kit, Warm and Bright, about thermal energy — heat, how it moves, and how to keep it in or let it out.

Experiments

  1. Keep a balloon from popping with water
  2. Test which materials move heat
  3. Design and build a drink insulator
  4. Use food coloring to see how heat works at the particle level

Experiment 1

Keep a balloon from popping with water

A little water in the balloon takes the heat, so the rubber doesn’t pop when the flame touches it.

Experiment 2

Test which materials move heat

Foil and clay in cups of water, with chocolate to see which material moves heat.

Experiment 3

Design and build a drink insulator

Build an insulator to keep a drink warm or cold, then test how well it works.
